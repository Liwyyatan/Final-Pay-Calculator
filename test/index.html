How every amount is calculated

The amounts

Monthly gross salary
Annual pay ÷ 14. If your annual pay covers only 12 payments, without the holiday and Christmas bonuses, it is ÷ 12 instead. The seniority allowance is added to it and, if you tick the shift allowance box, so is 15% of the base. Every amount that uses monthly pay uses this figure.
Compensation for years of service
Monthly gross salary × 2 months × years of service. Part-years count proportionally.
Fixed amount
The unemployment benefit of €1,387.15 per month × the number of months you choose, from 5 to 9. The proposal asks for 9 months, €12,484.35, under the expected 2027 legal criteria. It is added as it is.
2026 bonus
Annual pay × the bonus percentage (13% in the proposal, subject to the global grade). Paid in full.
Retention bonus
The amount you enter, for employees who were expected to meet the milestone criteria in 2027.
Commissions
The earned and outstanding sales and channel commissions you enter, including those settled after the termination.
Anniversary days
Unused anniversary days × the value of one day. One day is monthly pay × 12 ÷ 260.
Coverflex semester benefit
The €780 benefit in full, not reduced by the end date.
Holiday bonus
One month of gross salary × months worked this year ÷ 12, minus anything already paid. Months run from 1 January, or from the contract start if it is later, to the contract end.
Christmas bonus
Calculated the same way as the holiday bonus, except that it is paid with the November salary. For an end date in December, only the days worked in December count. For an end date in 2027, it is the pro-rata amount for 2027.
Unused vacation
Vacation days left × the value of one day. One day is monthly pay × 12 ÷ 260.
Annual leave entitlement
For a contract ending in 2027, the 28 days that vest on 1 January 2027 × the value of one day. It is a separate line from the unused days of this year.
Unused training hours
Training hours not provided in the last 5 years × the hourly rate. The hourly rate is monthly pay × 12 ÷ (52 × weekly working hours).
Notice period
60 calendar days is 2 months of monthly gross salary, fully paid as usual, also if the employee is released from duties. The contract end date is not moved.
Before deductions
All the amounts above added together.

The deductions

Social Security
11% of the amounts that count as pay: the 2026 bonus, retention bonus, commissions, anniversary days, Coverflex, unused vacation, annual leave entitlement, holiday and Christmas bonus and the notice pay. Nothing is taken from the compensation, the fixed amount or the training hours.
IRS on the compensation and the fixed amount
The compensation is tax-free up to your average monthly regular pay (monthly gross × 14 ÷ 12) × years of service. Only the part above that limit is taxed. The fixed amount uses whatever is left of the limit, and the rest is taxed. You can take the fixed amount out of IRS and Social Security with the checkbox.
IRS on everything else
Estimated with the 2026 Portuguese withholding table. The holiday and Christmas bonuses are taxed on their own, and the notice pay like a normal monthly salary. The other pay items and any taxed part of the severance are added on top of the month’s salary, so they are taxed at a higher rate. Dependents and the disability factor lower the result. It is an advance, and the final tax is settled in the annual return.
IRS Jovem
If ticked, the IRS on the pay items (everything except the compensation and the fixed amount) is reduced by the exempt share for the year of income: 100%, 75%, 50% or 25%. Social Security does not change, the taxed part of the severance is left out, and the yearly limit of €29,542.15 is assumed not to be used up already.
Lawyer’s fee
A fixed 2.5% of the total before deductions.
After deductions
Before deductions − Social Security − IRS − lawyer’s fee.
Frequently asked questions
How is my compensation calculated?

Your monthly gross salary, which is base pay plus the seniority allowance, is multiplied by 2 for each year of service. Part-years count proportionally. On top of that come the fixed amount (the unemployment benefit of €1,387.15 per month × 5 to 9 months, with 9 months in the proposal), the 2026 bonus and the other parcels shown in the proposed package.

What will I receive besides the compensation?

The fixed amount, the 2026 bonus, the retention bonus if you meet the 2027 milestone criteria, your commissions, unused anniversary days, the full €780 Coverflex semester benefit, the holiday and Christmas bonus, unused vacation and the 28-day annual leave entitlement, the training hours not provided in the last 5 years and the 60-day notice paid in full. Each one is its own line in the proposed package.

Is the compensation taxed?

Compensation from a collective dismissal carries no Social Security. For IRS, it is tax-free up to your average monthly pay × years of service, and only the part above that is taxed. The fixed amount may also be tax-free, depending on how it is set up. The reps will check this with the lawyer, and the checkbox “Take the fixed amount out of IRS and Social Security” shows the result without tax on it.

Why is the IRS on my final payment so high?

IRS is withheld on the whole month’s pay. Large one-off amounts such as the bonus or vacation pay are added to the month’s salary, so the month lands in a higher bracket and a higher rate applies. This is only an advance: the final tax is settled in your annual IRS return.

How are unused vacation days and the 28-day entitlement paid?

Each day is worth your monthly pay × 12 ÷ 260. If your contract ends in 2027, the 28 days that vest on 1 January 2027 are added as their own line, and in “Vacation days left” you only enter the unused days from this year.

What happens to my Christmas bonus if I leave in December?

The Christmas bonus is paid with the November salary, so for an end date in December only the days worked in December are calculated. For an end date in 2027 it is the pro-rata amount for 2027.

How many training hours am I owed?

You are entitled to 40 hours of training a year, and the proposal asks for the hours not provided in the last 5 years (up to 200 hours), minus the training you already completed. Each hour is paid at your monthly pay × 12 ÷ (52 × weekly hours). To know how many hours you are owed, check the provided Excel document on Discord.

How is the notice period paid?

The proposal asks for a minimum of 60 calendar days of notice for everyone, fully paid as usual, including when released from duties. The calculator shows it as 2 months of your monthly gross salary and does not move your contract end date.

What about stock options and health insurance?

Both are part of the proposal but have no fixed amount, so they are listed under the proposed package and are not in the total. For stock options, the proposal asks to protect vested rights and give favourable terms for unvested options: accelerated vesting, an exercise period of at least 3 years and clear individual statements. For health insurance, it asks for continued cover, including family members, until Pipedrive’s operations in Portugal effectively close.

Does IRS Jovem apply to me?

IRS Jovem is an IRS exemption for people aged 35 or under, for up to 10 years: 100% in the first year, 75% in years 2 to 4, 50% in years 5 to 7 and 25% in years 8 to 10. In the calculator you tick the option and choose your year. It reduces the IRS on the pay items only, not on the compensation or the fixed amount, and Social Security does not change.

What is the Lawyer’s fee?

A fixed 2.5% of the total package before deductions. It is shown as a separate deduction and does not change the Social Security or IRS amounts.

Will I receive unemployment benefit?

Everyone will be given the Social Security unemployment papers. Whether you actually receive the benefit depends on each person meeting the Social Security requirements, so it is decided case by case.

How exact is the calculator?

It gives estimates, not legal or tax advice. It uses the 2026 tax tables and does not apply the statutory caps in article 366 of the Labour Code. If something looks wrong or you have a suggestion, reach out to Sérgio R. on Discord.

      How the deductions are estimated

      Social Security is the employee’s 11% on pay items, except the unused training hours. Compensation from a collective dismissal is outside the Social Security base, so it carries no contribution. If you tick the option to take the fixed amount or the Coverflex benefit out of IRS and Social Security, it gets neither. The lawyer’s fee still applies to the total of the whole package.

      IRS follows the 2026 mainland withholding tables (Despacho 233-A/2026). The holiday and Christmas bonuses are withheld on their own, and the notice pay like a normal monthly salary. Vacation pay, anniversary days, the annual leave entitlement, training pay, the 2026 bonus, retention bonus, commissions, Coverflex and any taxable severance are added to the month’s salary, which is assumed to be paid in the same payment, so they are withheld at a higher rate.

      The severance is exempt from IRS up to average monthly regular pay × years of service. The exemption is lost, for example, if the person works for the same employer again within 24 months.

      The lawyer’s fee is a fixed 2.5% of the total of the whole package and does not change the Social Security or IRS estimate.

      For each dependent with a disability of 60% or more, €84.82 a month (€42.41 if married with two incomes) is added to the amount deducted from the withholding. Your employer can multiply it by up to 3, or up to 6 if married with two incomes, and the calculator uses the factor you enter.

      Not covered: the Azores and Madeira tables, the tables for taxpayers who have a disability themselves, and the separate exemption for voluntary performance bonuses. Withholding is an advance, and the final tax is settled in the annual return.

      Estimates only, not legal or tax advice. The proposal’s 2 months per year are applied to the whole length of service, and the statutory caps in article 366 of the Labour Code are not applied. The notice period is shown as a separate parcel and does not move the contract end date.
