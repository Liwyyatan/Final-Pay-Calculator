    How the deductions are estimated

    Social Security is the employee’s 11% on pay items. Severance from a collective dismissal is outside the Social Security base, so it carries no contribution.

    IRS follows the 2026 mainland withholding tables (Despacho 233-A/2026). The holiday and Christmas bonuses are withheld on their own. Vacation pay, training pay, the annual bonus and any taxable severance are added to the month’s salary, which is assumed to be paid in the same payment, so they are withheld at a higher rate.

    The severance is exempt from IRS up to average monthly regular pay × years of service. The exemption is lost, for example, if the person works for the same employer again within 24 months.

    The additional deduction is applied to the total you choose and does not change the Social Security or IRS estimate.

    For each dependent with a disability of 60% or more, €84.82 a month (€42.41 if married with two incomes) is added to the amount deducted from the withholding. Your employer can multiply it by up to 3, or up to 6 if married with two incomes, and the calculator uses the factor you enter.

    Not covered: the Azores and Madeira tables, the tables for taxpayers who have a disability themselves, IRS Jovem, and the separate exemption for voluntary performance bonuses. Withholding is an advance, and the final tax is settled in the annual return.

    Estimates only, not legal or tax advice. The three-month minimum and the statutory caps in article 366 of the Labour Code are not applied. The days per year are applied to the whole length of service, without the transitional split for service before 1 May 2023.
