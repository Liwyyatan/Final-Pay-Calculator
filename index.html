How every amount is calculated

The amounts

Monthly base salary
Annual pay ÷ 14. If your annual pay covers only 12 payments, without the holiday and Christmas bonuses, it is ÷ 12 instead. If you tick the shift allowance box, 15% is added, and every amount below uses that higher monthly pay.
Severance for years of service
Monthly base salary ÷ 30 gives one day of pay. Multiply it by the severance days per year (14 + 16 by default) and by the years of service. Part-years count proportionally.
Additional severance
The fixed amount you enter, added as it is.
Unused vacation
Vacation days left × the value of one day. One day is monthly pay × 12 ÷ 260.
Unused training hours
Training hours owed × the hourly rate. The hourly rate is base salary × 12 ÷ (52 × weekly working hours).
Holiday bonus
One month of base salary × months worked this year ÷ 12, minus anything already paid. Months run from 1 January, or from the contract start if it is later, to the contract end.
Christmas bonus
Calculated the same way as the holiday bonus, except that it is paid with the November salary. For an end date in December, only the days worked in December count. For an end date in 2027, it is the pro-rata amount for 2027.
Annual bonus
The amount you enter, paid in full.
Before deductions
All the amounts above added together.

The deductions

Social Security
11% of the amounts that count as pay: unused vacation, holiday bonus, Christmas bonus and annual bonus. Nothing is taken from the severance or from the training hours.
IRS on the severance
Severance is tax-free up to your average monthly regular pay (base salary × 14 ÷ 12) × years of service. Only the part above that limit is taxed. You can also take the additional severance out of IRS and Social Security with the checkbox.
IRS on everything else
Estimated with the 2026 Portuguese withholding table. The holiday and Christmas bonuses are taxed on their own. Vacation, training, the annual bonus and any taxed severance are added on top of the month’s salary, so they are taxed at a higher rate. Dependents and the disability factor lower the result. It is an advance, and the final tax is settled in the annual return.
IRS Jovem
If ticked, the IRS on the pay items (vacation, training, holiday and Christmas bonus, annual bonus) is reduced by the exempt share for the year of income: 100%, 75%, 50% or 25%. Social Security does not change, the taxed part of the severance is left out, and the yearly limit of €29,542.15 is assumed not to be used up already.
Lawyer’s fee
A fixed 2.5% of the total before deductions.
After deductions
Before deductions − Social Security − IRS − lawyer’s fee.
Frequently asked questions
How is my severance calculated?

One day of pay is your monthly pay ÷ 30. That is multiplied by 14 + 16 days for each year of service, and by your years of service. Part-years count proportionally. In practice this equals one month of pay for each year served.

What will I receive besides the severance?

The additional severance amount, your unused vacation days, the training hours you are owed, the holiday and Christmas bonus for the part of the year you worked, and the annual bonus. Each one is shown as its own line in the final account.

Is the severance taxed?

Severance from a collective dismissal carries no Social Security. For IRS, it is tax-free up to your average monthly pay × years of service, and only the part above that is taxed. The additional severance may also be tax-free, depending on how it is set up. The reps will check this with the lawyer, and the checkbox “Take the additional severance out of IRS and Social Security” shows the result without tax on it.

Why is the IRS on my final payment so high?

IRS is withheld on the whole month’s pay. Large one-off amounts such as the annual bonus or vacation pay are added to the month’s salary, so the month lands in a higher bracket and a higher rate applies. This is only an advance: the final tax is settled in your annual IRS return.

How are unused vacation days paid?

Each day is worth your monthly pay × 12 ÷ 260. If your contract ends in 2027, add the 22 vacation days that vest on 1 January 2027 to “Vacation days left”, together with any unused days from this year.

What happens to my Christmas bonus if I leave in December?

The Christmas bonus is paid with the November salary, so for an end date in December only the days worked in December are calculated. For an end date in 2027 it is the pro-rata amount for 2027.

How many training hours am I owed?

You are entitled to 40 hours of training a year, and the hours not provided are paid when the contract ends. The calculation looks at the last 3 years (up to 120 hours), minus the training you already completed. Each hour is paid at your monthly pay × 12 ÷ (52 × weekly hours). To know how many hours you are owed, check the provided Excel document on Discord.

Does IRS Jovem apply to me?

IRS Jovem is an IRS exemption for people aged 35 or under, for up to 10 years: 100% in the first year, 75% in years 2 to 4, 50% in years 5 to 7 and 25% in years 8 to 10. In the calculator you tick the option and choose your year. It reduces the IRS on the pay items only, not on the severance, and Social Security does not change.

What is the Lawyer’s fee?

A fixed 2.5% of the total package before deductions. It is shown as a separate deduction and does not change the Social Security or IRS amounts.

Will there be garden leave?

Garden leave means being released from work while still being paid. It is on the table, but it is not known yet who will benefit from it.

Will I receive unemployment benefit?

Everyone will be given the Social Security unemployment papers. Whether you actually receive the benefit depends on each person meeting the Social Security requirements, so it is decided case by case.

How exact is the calculator?

It gives estimates, not legal or tax advice. It uses the 2026 tax tables and does not apply the statutory caps in article 366 of the Labour Code. If something looks wrong or you have a suggestion, reach out to Sérgio R. on Discord.

      How the deductions are estimated

      Social Security is the employee’s 11% on pay items, except the unused training hours. Severance from a collective dismissal is outside the Social Security base, so it carries no contribution. If you tick the option to take the additional severance out of IRS and Social Security, it gets no IRS. The lawyer’s fee still applies to the total of the whole package.

      IRS follows the 2026 mainland withholding tables (Despacho 233-A/2026). The holiday and Christmas bonuses are withheld on their own. Vacation pay, training pay, the annual bonus and any taxable severance are added to the month’s salary, which is assumed to be paid in the same payment, so they are withheld at a higher rate.

      The severance is exempt from IRS up to average monthly regular pay × years of service. The exemption is lost, for example, if the person works for the same employer again within 24 months.

      The lawyer’s fee is a fixed 2.5% of the total of the whole package and does not change the Social Security or IRS estimate.

      For each dependent with a disability of 60% or more, €84.82 a month (€42.41 if married with two incomes) is added to the amount deducted from the withholding. Your employer can multiply it by up to 3, or up to 6 if married with two incomes, and the calculator uses the factor you enter.

      Not covered: the Azores and Madeira tables, the tables for taxpayers who have a disability themselves, and the separate exemption for voluntary performance bonuses. Withholding is an advance, and the final tax is settled in the annual return.

      Estimates only, not legal or tax advice. The three-month minimum and the statutory caps in article 366 of the Labour Code are not applied. The days per year are applied to the whole length of service, without the transitional split for service before 1 May 2023.
